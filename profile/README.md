## 👋 About GoDojo

GoDojo is an AI-powered sales co-pilot that helps sales teams perform better in every conversation.

From real-time coaching and meeting intelligence to actionable insights and follow-ups, GoDojo helps sales professionals spend less time on administrative work and more time closing deals.

## ✨ What GoDojo Does

| 📅 Before the Call | 🎙️ During the Call | 📊 After the Call |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar integration, company context, and AI-powered meeting preparation. | Live transcription, real-time coaching, objection handling, and BANT/MEDDICC tracking. | AI-generated summaries, call analysis, sales scorecards, actionable next steps, and follow-up emails. |

## ⚡ Key Features

- **🧠 Real-Time AI Coaching:** Get contextual suggestions and insights during live sales conversations.
- **🎙️ Meeting Intelligence:** Automatically capture, transcribe, and analyze sales meetings.
- **💬 Ask Dojo:** Ask AI questions about previous meetings and receive context-aware answers.
- **📚 Company Knowledge:** Connect company and product context to generate more relevant insights.
- **📈 Sales Performance Insights:** Identify coaching opportunities, track deal progress, and improve sales effectiveness.
- **🛡️ Privacy-Focused Experience:** A discreet desktop assistant designed to work alongside meetings without requiring a meeting bot.

## 💻 Supported Platforms

GoDojo is a cross-platform desktop application available for:

- 🪟 **Windows**
- 🍎 **macOS**
- 🐧 **Linux**

GoDojo works alongside popular meeting platforms, including **Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams**, and other calling tools.
